# KinPlay Activities

## Animal Mirror Parade
- ID: `animal_mirror_parade`
- Type: activity
- Modes: quick_play, pick_a_game
- Quick categories: outdoor_adventures, get_energy_out, quality_time
- Participant suitability: both
- Age: 2–8
- Duration: 8 min
- Energy: medium
- Summary: Take turns leading silly animal movements while everyone mirrors the leader.
- Materials: none
- Safety tags: parent_supervision, movement, no_materials, sibling_friendly
- Setup: Clear a small safe space for walking and wiggling.
- Play steps: Choose one leader. / The leader moves like an animal while everyone copies. / Switch leaders after three animals.
- Parent note: Use gentle movements for younger children and avoid jumping near furniture.
- Variations: Whisper the animal for a quiet round. / Let each child invent a brand-new animal.

- Summary: Build a ready-to-start family story with the next letter of the alphabet.

## Washable Painting Shapes
- ID: `washable_painting_shapes`
- Type: activity
- Modes: pick_a_game, calm_down
- Quick categories: quiet_games, quality_time
- Participant suitability: both
- Age: 2–8
- Duration: 15 min
- Energy: calm
- Summary: Paint ready-made shapes with washable, non-toxic paint, protected surfaces, and adult supervision.
- Materials: paper, washable non-toxic children’s paint, paintbrushes, table covering, water and cloth
- Safety tags: parent_supervision, small_objects, quiet, sibling_friendly
- Setup: Adult supervises, confirms the children’s paint is washable and non-toxic, and covers the work surface and floor for protection. / Put out a small amount of paint, brushes, paper, water, and a cloth; draw a large circle, square, and triangle.
- Play steps: Paint the circle with dots, the square with stripes, and the triangle with zigzags. / Trade brushes or colors and add one shape to each other’s page. / Place paintings flat to dry, close the paint, and wash hands and the protected surface together.
- Parent note: Supervise continuously; keep paint and rinse water out of mouths and clean spills promptly.
- Variations: Make a shape family with faces. / Use one color and mix lighter or darker shapes.
