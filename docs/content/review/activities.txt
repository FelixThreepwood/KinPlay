# KinPlay Activities

## Animal Mirror Parade
- ID: `animal_mirror_parade`
- Type: activity
- Modes: quick_play, pick_a_game
- Quick categories: outdoor_adventures, get_energy_out, quality_time
- Participant suitability: both
- Minimum age: 2+
- Duration: 8 min
- Energy: medium
- Summary: Take turns leading silly animal movements while everyone mirrors the leader.
- Format group: animal_play
- Materials: none
- Safety tags: parent_supervision, movement, no_materials, sibling_friendly
- Setup: Pick a spot, then take turns moving like animals.
- Play steps: Choose one leader. / The leader moves like an animal while everyone copies. / Switch leaders after three animals.
- Parent note: Play another round. Each player leads as a different animal.
- Variations: Whisper the animal for a quiet round. / Let each child invent a brand-new animal.

## Alphabet Story
- ID: `story_spark_circle`
- Type: activity
- Modes: quick_play, pick_a_game
- Quick categories: quiet_games, dinner_table, brain_games, quality_time
- Participant suitability: group
- Minimum age: 4+
- Duration: 12 min
- Energy: calm
- Summary: Build a ready-to-start family story with the next letter of the alphabet.
- Format group: story_circle
- Materials: none
- Safety tags: quiet, no_materials, reading_help, sibling_friendly
- Setup: Sit where everyone can hear; begin with A, or use the shorter A-through-F round.
- Play steps: Adult reads the ready-made start: “A tiny alligator found a bright balloon.” / Continue with B: “Bouncy bears brought blueberry biscuits.” / Continue with C: “Curious cats came closer.” / Then take turns with D, E, and F; short phrases, sounds, or one word all count. / Finish the story after F, or keep going through the alphabet if everyone wants more.
- Parent note: Offer the sample sentence or a word beginning with the next letter so no child has to invent alone.
- Variations: Ready animal round: alligator, bear, cat, dog, elephant, frog. / Ready food round: apple, banana, carrot, doughnut, egg, fruit. / For ages 2–3, say the next letter and animal together; the child can make its sound.
